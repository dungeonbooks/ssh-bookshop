Dungeon Books

The monthly book club shelf, in your terminal.

Open the shop

ssh shop.dungeonbooks.com

What this is

Every month we pick a science fiction or fantasy book for the shop's book club. This is that shelf, browsable over SSH. Prices come from the same Square catalog as the store, so what you see is what's actually on the shelf in the shop.

Any SSH key gets you in. Nothing is installed, and browsing is anonymous. Buying hands you off to dungeonbooks.com.
