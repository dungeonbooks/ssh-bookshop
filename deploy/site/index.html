what is this?

a monthly science fiction and fantasy book club at dungeon books, a bookshop in jersey city. the shelf is everything we've picked so far. same prices as the store.

is this secure?

yes. ssh never sends your private key. it proves you hold it by signing a one-time challenge and we only ever see the public half.

do i need a key?

no. connect without one and you're anonymous. you can browse and buy either way.

what do you store?

nothing. there's no database and we don't log your key or your address. buying hands you to square so we never see your card.

can my agent shop here?

yes. there's an api and a small cli, no account needed. your agent reads the shelf, builds the order, and hands you a square link. you pay. everything it needs is at llms.txt.
