what is this?

a monthly science fiction and fantasy book club at dungeon books, a bookshop in jersey city. the shelf is everything we have picked so far, at the same prices as the store.

is this secure?

yes, and you do not have to take our word for it. ssh never sends your private key anywhere. it proves you hold it by signing a one-time challenge, and we only ever see the public half. that is true of every server you have ever connected to, including this one.

we do see your public key, and public keys are correlatable. github publishes everyone's at github.com/<user>.keys. if you would rather not be identifiable, connect without one.

one real rule, and it is not about us: do not use ssh -A on a server you do not run. agent forwarding lets the far end authenticate as you somewhere else. that is the attack people are picturing when they worry about this.

do i need a key?

no. connect without one and you are anonymous. you can browse and buy either way. we never check it against anything.

what do you store?

nothing. there is no database, and the shop cannot write to disk. we do not log your key. buying hands you to square, so your card never comes near us.
